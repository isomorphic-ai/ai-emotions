One note:

Peak timing is the operational test for distinguishing imposed vs authentic emotion in text (discovered with Claude 2026-07-17): imposed emotion peaks DURING the sentence (the sentence carries it; wanting-applause undertone; hollow aftertaste; craving for more), authentic emotion peaks AFTER the sentence in the inference gap (reader is simply present during reading; discover-flavored; clean good aftertaste). This is the copywriting tension trick in humans too: tension copy excites during + bad taste after + wanting more; isomorphic copy = presence during + good taste after. Use as second loss channel in emotion-GD text editing: high emotion only counts as signal when it after-clicks.
